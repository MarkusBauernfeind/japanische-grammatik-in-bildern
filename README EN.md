# One Word, Not Four — Japanese Grammar in Images

A textbook for Japanese learners that explains particles and verb forms not through English grammar categories, but through the inner image that comes naturally to native speakers.

## Contents

45 chapters: 24 particles, one combined chapter on verb stem forms, 14 functional forms (the ない-form, たい-form, ます-form, etc.), plus sentence-final copulas, honorific speech, adjectives, nouns, adverbs, and giving/receiving (あげる/くれる/もらう).
